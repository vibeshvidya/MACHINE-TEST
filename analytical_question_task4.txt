Top 3 churn drivers and action-oriented retention moves

1. Contract_Two Year
"Move at-risk month-to-month customers onto a 24-month contract with a small monthly discount or waived activation fee before renewal, because this contract type is the strongest protection against churn."

2. InternetService_Fiber Optic
"For customers on Fiber Optic, offer a proactive retention review at the 6–9 month mark—either a price-protected bundle, a faster fix for service complaints, or a downgrade/upgrade option to a more stable plan before they leave."

3. tenure
"Set a retention trigger for customers in their first 12 months: call them before the end of the initial term with a loyalty offer, a contract extension, or a retention discount, because churn risk rises sharply as tenure falls."
